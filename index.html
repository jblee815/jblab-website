About

Our laboratory began in February 2007 in the Department of Physics at POSTECH, in Science Building III. We use single-molecule biophysics techniques to study various biological problems, and we develop new techniques when our questions call for them.

We are always welcoming passionate and creative students and researchers. If you want to ask bold questions about how life works, we invite you to grow with us.
